UMANG — running India's national super-app

Flagship · National platform

Digital India Corporation · NeGD (MeitY) — Senior DevOps Engineer, Nov 2025 – present

The brief: own the AWS platform behind UMANG — the national super-app for citizen services at national scale — alongside DevOps delivery for NITI Aayog flagships UPMS, NITI For States, and NGO Darpan (HLD/LLD, deployments, CI/CD).

What was done

  • Run the hybrid EKS + ECS estate behind multi-AZ Envoy ingress, with KEDA-driven autoscaling and releases gated on SLOs and error budgets rather than calendar dates.
  • Led the lift-and-shift of MyScheme into UMANG — environment parity, secure VPC peering, CI/CD wiring, FinOps-driven cost optimization, and production cutover with zero user-facing downtime.
  • Standardised OpenTelemetry instrumentation and shared SRE runbooks across the microservice estate — including integrations with EPFO, DigiYatra, DigiLocker and NSSO — so incidents are diagnosed from shared signals, not tribal knowledge.

KEY DECISIONS & ALTERNATIVES
Hybrid EKS + ECS, not a big-bang replatformA national citizen service cannot pause. New workloads land on EKS while stable services stay on ECS — migration risk is taken in slices, never all at once.
Error-budget release gatesReleases are gated on measured SLOs and error budgets, not calendar pressure — the mechanism that keeps release trains boring, in the best sense.
KEDA event-driven autoscalingCitizen traffic spikes around government deadlines; scaling follows events and queues, not just CPU averages.
LIMITATIONS & LESSONS
LimitationsPlatform-level metrics are team outcomes on a shared national platform; change windows are governed by multiple stakeholders, which trades speed for safety.
LessonObservability before automation: incident response improved when telemetry and runbooks were standardised across services — shared signals, not more tooling.
LessonAn error budget is a negotiation tool — it turns "ship vs stability" arguments into arithmetic.
