Zero-downtime AWS migration for regulated fintech

Fintech · Migration

Creditech Solutions (AlphaMoney) — Lead DevOps & MLOps Engineer, Feb 2024 – Aug 2025

The brief: migrate a regulated fintech workload on AWS — a domain where downtime means compliance risk — and modernize the platform's cost base without touching customer experience.

What was done

  • Executed the migration as an active-active EKS move with Route 53 weighted traffic shifts and Terraform-managed landing zones — traffic moved in measured, reversible steps.
  • Replaced manual provisioning with reusable Terraform modules, Karpenter-driven node consolidation and right-sized RDS PostgreSQL tiers — cost treated as an architectural input, reviewed at every release.
  • Extended platform and DevOps practices across parent-group ventures — AlphaCorp and Alpha Design Studio — delivering cloud-hosted ERP and a secure corporate intranet that standardized infrastructure across three sister organizations.
