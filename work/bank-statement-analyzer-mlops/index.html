MLOps in production: Bank Statement Analyzer

Fintech · MLOps

Creditech Solutions (AlphaMoney) — Lead DevOps & MLOps Engineer

The brief: ship a production ML system that ingests and classifies bank statements for credit decisioning — a pipeline that had to be reliable, observable, and cheap to operate day after day.

What was done

  • Shipped a production Bank Statement Analyzer ingesting and classifying thousands of statements daily — a FastAPI inference service backed by Airflow-orchestrated ETL and feature pipelines, an MLflow model registry, and Kubernetes-native autoscaling.
  • Designed for MLOps maturity: versioned models in a registry, reproducible feature pipelines, and inference that scales with demand instead of sitting over-provisioned.
  • Applied the same discipline on consulting mandates — GPU workload orchestration and AI/ML CI/CD for Aftershoot, and an 8-week Kubeflow + MLflow real-time & batch inference HLD/LLD for ZS Associates.
