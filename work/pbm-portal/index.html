PBM Portal — Ministry of Finance, staging to production

Government · Finance platform

NeGD (MeitY) · Ministry of Finance programme — Senior DevOps Engineer

The brief: own DevOps for the PBM programme-management portal end-to-end — staging operations day to day, and a full production go-live on the ministry's secured on-premise network.

What was done

  • Run staging + production end-to-end: Dockerized NestJS backend + Angular frontend behind Apache, PostgreSQL, scripted deployments with pre-deploy database backups and post-deploy seed/permission verification on every release.
  • Took the portal live in production on the ministry's on-prem network — frontend, backend, database and an AI service wired across the LAN under Sectigo TLS — with automated weekly backups of files and both databases to S3-compatible storage.
  • Shipped an AI service (LLM + embeddings) for proposal summaries, including a bulk-summary API (up to 100 proposals per job with progress tracking), and closed VAPT/SAST findings through audited rounds — RSA-OAEP password encryption, OTP hardening, permission-model fixes.
