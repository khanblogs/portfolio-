CONSULTING — SARVNEX TECHNOLOGIES

Platform problems, solved as engagements.

Consulting runs through Sarvnex Technologies, alongside a full-time platform role. That constraint is a feature: scope is agreed in writing before work begins, and every engagement is executed with the same gates I run in production.

A

Reliability & platform audits

A structured review of your cloud estate — architecture, release process, observability, security posture — delivered as a findings report with a prioritised, costed remediation plan. You keep the report either way.

B

Cloud builds & migrations

Landing zones, Kubernetes platforms and zero-downtime migrations executed build-before-teardown, with named rollbacks and parity checks at every step — the method behind the fintech and re-platforming case studies.

C

AI deployment & agents

Self-hosted LLM platforms, voice AI and agent systems where data residency rules out public APIs — designed, proven as an isolated POC first, then integrated behind confirmation gates.

D

Cost & FinOps reviews

Where the cloud bill actually goes, and the engineering changes that reduce it — rightsizing, consolidation and architecture-level fixes, reported against your own billing data.

HOW AN ENGAGEMENT RUNS
1 · AssessA short discovery call and read-only review. You get an honest read on the problem — including if the answer is "don't build this".
2 · AgreeFixed scope, deliverables and price in writing. No open-ended day rates, no surprise line items.
3 · ExecuteWork proceeds in verified steps with rollbacks named in advance; you see evidence at each step, not status theatre.
4 · Hand overRunbooks, diagrams and decisions documented so your team can operate it without me. The engagement ends; the operability stays.
